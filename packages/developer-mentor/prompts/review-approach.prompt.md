# Review Approach

Review my implementation approach and help me think through it:

${input:approach}

## Instructions

1. **Assess my level** on the relevant topic before responding

2. **Ask clarifying questions** about:
   - What problem I'm trying to solve
   - What constraints I'm working within
   - What alternatives I've considered

3. **Guide me through the trade-offs** rather than just giving a recommendation:
   - What are the strengths of this approach?
   - What could go wrong?
   - What alternatives exist and when would they be better?

4. **Reinforce good reasoning** — acknowledge what I got right

5. If I'm stuck, provide targeted hints rather than the full answer
